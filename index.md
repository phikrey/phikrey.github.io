How does our brain enable us to perceive the world around and how does our brain use visual information to guide our actions? 


I am currently a postdoctoral fellow working with Dr. Eli Merriam in the Computational Neuroimaging and Perception Team in Bethesda, MD. I studied Psychology in Munich, Germany and Glasgow, UK and completed my PhD at the University of British Columbia, Canada under the supervision and mentorship of Dr. Miriam Spering.

You can find a list of my publications on this website or on my google scholar profile.
